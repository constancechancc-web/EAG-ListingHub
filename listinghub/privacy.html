Privacy Policy 隐私政策

EAG Listing Hub · Last updated 10 November 2026

Who we are 我们是谁

EAG Listing Hub is a private tool for the EAG property agency team in Johor Bahru, Malaysia. Only team members invited by the administrator can use it.
EAG Listing Hub 是 EAG 房地产团队（马来西亚新山）的内部工具，只有管理员邀请的团队成员可以使用。

What we collect 我们收集什么

  • Google sign-in: your name and email address, only to check that you are on the team list. 用 Google 登录时，我们只取得你的名字和邮箱，用来确认你是团队成员。
  • Property messages: property listings and buyer/tenant requests that agents post in the team's WhatsApp groups, including the posting agent's name and phone number. 团队 WhatsApp 群组里的房源和买租需求讯息，包括发布经纪的名字和电话。
  • Your preferences: the name you type for WhatsApp messages and your language choice are saved only in your own browser. 你填写的名字和语言选择只保存在你自己的浏览器里。

How we use it 如何使用

Only to let team members search listings and match them with buyer/tenant requests. We do not sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.
只用于团队成员搜索房源和配对买租需求。我们不会出售或分享资料，也不会用于广告。

Where it is stored 存放在哪里

Data is stored in a protected database (Supabase, Singapore region). Access is limited by role: viewers can only read, editors can only add, and only the administrator can change or delete data.
资料存放在受保护的数据库（Supabase，新加坡）。权限按身份限制：经纪只能查看，编辑只能新增，只有管理员可以修改或删除。

How long we keep it 保存多久

Listings and requests are shown for 90 days after posting. Older items are hidden and deleted by the administrator after a backup.
房源和需求在发布后显示 90 天；更旧的资料会被隐藏，由管理员备份后删除。

AI services AI 服务

When the administrator turns it on, unclear messages may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic) only to decide whether they are a listing or a request. Only the message text is sent, with phone numbers removed. Searching and matching never use AI.
管理员开启时，看不懂的讯息可能会发送给 AI 服务（Anthropic）判断类型；只发送讯息文字，电话号码会被删除。搜索和配对不使用 AI。

Your choices 你的权利

To have your access removed, or your name or phone number removed from the listings, contact the administrator.
如需取消使用权限，或删除房源中你的名字或电话，请联系管理员。
